Frequently asked

Straight answers before you sign up.

What does The Property Tax Department actually do?+

We review your property, identify the strongest appeal argument, prepare the evidence, and file the appeal before the deadline — so if your assessment is too high, it gets corrected.

What is a property tax appeal, exactly?+

It's a request to reduce your property's assessed value — not the tax rate, your school district, or local spending. It's based on market value, comparables, uniformity, and other relevant evidence.

Should I even bother getting my assessment reviewed?+

If you own property in Cook or Lake County, it's worth checking — especially if your assessment recently increased, you bought recently, or similar nearby homes are assessed lower.
